Why official

Workarounds work until the number gets blocked

Many organizations send messages from an employee's phone or through unofficial tools. That works until the number is blocked in the middle of a busy period, and the entire client history sits on someone's personal phone. WhatsApp's official business API solves both problems.

What it does

Organized communication, no personal phones

Approved messages and templates

Send messages and templates pre-approved by WhatsApp to one person or to a group.

Automatic alerts

Messages that go out on their own when something happens in the system: a payment, a reminder, an approval or a status change.

Smart bot

AI answers to questions, document collection, identification and analysis, and handoff to a person when needed.

Connected to your systems

Messages go out from your CRM or management system, not from a separate app.

Connecting your account

You connect through Eitan Connect

Your organization connects its WhatsApp Business account through Eitan Connect, our app on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. During setup, a Meta window asks you to authorize Eitan Connect to manage your WhatsApp Business account. The account and phone number remain owned by your organization, and you can revoke access at any time from your Meta Business settings.
