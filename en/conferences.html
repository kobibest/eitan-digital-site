Ready-to-deploy system

Conferences and retreats without dozens of spreadsheets

For organizations that run large events: registration for participants and their families, room and flight allocation, preferences, accessibility and payments, all in one clear place. The system grew out of the Israel Mortgage Advisors Association's conferences and is now used by several organizations.

Book a demo What it does

Why use a system at all

Big conferences fall apart on the small details

A family member who never registered, a room assigned twice, a passport name that doesn't match the ticket, a failed payment nobody noticed. When everything is spread across spreadsheets, phone calls and email, each of these is only a matter of time.

What it does

The whole event, in one clear process

Participant and family registration

Registration under the participant's name, with spouse, phone number and children's ages.

Preferences and accessibility

Strict kosher, vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, accessibility needs and free-text notes.

Room allocation and inventory

Tracking room inventory and assigning rooms without double bookings.

Flights

Name in English as it appears on the passport and date of birth when there's a flight, plus seat inventory tracking.

Performers and sponsors

Manual room registration for performers and sponsors alongside regular attendees.

Failed payment alerts

When a registrant's payment doesn't go through, the system flags it right away, not a week before the event.

Rollout

Adapted to your event

Every organization runs events a little differently. So rollout starts with understanding how your event works, and only then setting up the system.

  1. Discovery

    We map out the event: audience, rooms, flights, payments and who handles what.

  2. Configuration

    We set up the registration form, fields and allocation rules around your needs.

  3. Setup and testing

    We launch the system and test it before registration opens.

  4. Support

    I'm available throughout the registration period and up to the event itself.
