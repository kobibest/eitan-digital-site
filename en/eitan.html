The challenge

The firm grew. The process didn't.

In a small advisory firm, the advisor remembers everything. Once the firm grows to several advisors, administrative staff and hundreds of open files, memory is no longer enough. Documents go missing, tasks slip, and nobody knows exactly where each file stands.

What it does

From the first lead to closing

Automatic document reading

Loan balance statements and bank pre-approvals are read automatically, with no manual typing.

Client files and tasks

Every file, document and task in one place, with reminders and dependencies between stages.

Leads and campaigns

Managing leads from the moment they come in, and tracking the campaigns that brought them.

A landing page for every advisor

Built-in landing pages that send inquiries straight into the system.

Multi-office structure

Teams, permissions and separation between offices, so the system also fits a network of firms.

Mobile access

Full access from the phone, even when the advisor is in a meeting or at the bank.

Rollout in large firms

Not just a system, but a structured move to it

In a large firm, changing the way people work matters as much as the system itself. That's why rollout starts with the firm, not with the software.

  1. Mapping the firm

    Understanding how the firm works today: who does what, and where things fall through.

  2. Data migration

    Moving existing files and clients over without losing their history.

  3. Team and permissions

    Setting up users, roles and the firm's workflows.

  4. Training and support

    Training the whole team and staying with them until the system is part of the daily routine.

FAQ

Questions about rolling out Eitan CRM

What is Eitan CRM?

Eitan CRM is a CRM for mortgage advisory firms, built from the fieldwork of mortgage advisors: leads, client files, documents, banks and the team. It runs in large advisory firms, and hundreds of advisors work in it.

Which documents does it read automatically?

Loan balance statements and bank pre-approvals are read automatically, with no manual typing, and the data goes into the client file.

Does it fit a network of several offices?

Yes. It has a multi-office structure with teams, permissions and separation between offices.

What does rollout in a large firm include?

Mapping how the firm works today, moving existing files and clients over without losing their history, setting up users, roles and workflows, and training the whole team with support until the system is part of the daily routine.
